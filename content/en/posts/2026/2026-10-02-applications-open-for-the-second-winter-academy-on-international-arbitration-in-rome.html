The Winter Academy on International Arbitration returns to Rome for its second edition, from 25 to 29 January 2027, bringing together emerging lawyers and leading practitioners for an intensive programme combining advanced arbitration training, career insights and professional networking. Organised by Luiss School of Law in conjunction with the Centre for International Dispute Settlement (CIDS) in Geneva, the Academy provides advanced instruction on key issues in international commercial and investment arbitration, while offering participants direct exposure to the professional realities of international arbitration practice. Applications for the 2026/2027 academic year are currently open.

Learn from leading practitioners

A
distinctive feature of the Winter Academy is its practitioner-led approach. Sessions with ten arbitration partners from international law firms combine substantive teaching with candid conversations about their professional journeys and the realities of building a career in international arbitration . Led by Professor
Zachary Douglas, these exchanges explore the qualities and qualifications
important for young lawyers entering international arbitration, provide an
insider's perspective on law firm recruitment, and give participants the
opportunity to put their own questions directly to practitioners. Following
these conversations, each practitioner provides an in-depth analysis of a
particular topic in international arbitration, with an emphasis on how the
issues arise and are addressed in actual cases. The aim is to provide a
practical perspective that goes beyond what can be learned from academic texts
alone. The programme also includes presentations by legal counsel from the
International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes (ICSID) and the
Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA). In this
way, the Winter Academy connects advanced legal training with a better
understanding of how international arbitration works in practice and how
careers in the field are built.

An international community

The
inaugural edition of the Academy in 2026 welcomed 45 participants from 17
countries and 25 universities, bringing together primarily students alongside
practising lawyers.

The Academy
places particular emphasis on interaction and networking, allowing participants
to develop connections with both practitioners and peers who share their
interest in international dispute settlement.

Building on
the success of the first edition, this year's Academy will once again offer

connect

arbitration.

Who can

The Winter
Academy is open to candidates holding a Bachelor's degree, Master's degree or

in one of these academic pathways who have not yet obtained their degree may
also participate, subject to available places. These participants will receive
a certificate of attendance but will not be eligible for ECTS credits. Applicants
are required to submit:


a copy of a
valid identity document;


an academic
transcript;



and a CV.
